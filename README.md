# Responsible Data Science for Finance (Mini MBA)

Four-day block-delivered course that combines regulatory literacy, responsible AI practice, and hands-on analytics for treasury and credit applications. Materials are optimized for Google Colab so cohorts can work from managed laptops while instructors provide pre-exported Bloomberg datasets.

## Course Flow
- **Day 1 – Responsible Foundations & Data Controls:** ethics, regulation, ESG data validation notebook.
- **Day 2 – Causal & Fair Modelling:** credit-spread treatment effects and fairness mitigation lab.
- **Day 3 – Bayesian Scenario Analysis:** PyMC-based yield curve stress testing.
- **Day 4 – Capstone Sprint:** build a toy fintech analytics app (Gradio/Streamlit) that recommends ESG-compliant allocations.

## What We Provide
- Bloomberg CSV extracts for ESG scores, credit spreads, macro series, and yield curves (distributed privately to participants ahead of time).
- Colab-ready notebooks under `labs/` plus a starter Streamlit app under `app/`.
- Requirements file for local execution if needed (`pip install -r requirements.txt`).

## Prerequisites for Applicants
1. **Finance knowledge:** working understanding of fixed income instruments, credit spreads, and basic regulatory acronyms (Basel, SFDR, ECOA). Prior treasury, credit, or fintech experience is ideal but not mandatory.
2. **Statistics background:** prior coursework in regression/econometrics, able to interpret confidence intervals, recognize confounding, and discuss Bayesian vs. classical inference at a conceptual level.
3. **Programming proficiency:** intermediate Python (pandas, plotting, scikit-learn). Should be comfortable editing Jupyter/Colab notebooks and running `pip` installs. Experience with Git or version control is helpful but not required.
4. **Machine learning familiarity:** exposure to supervised learning concepts (train/test splits, ROC/AUC, overfitting) and basic awareness of responsible AI/fairness terminology.
5. **Data handling discipline:** experience importing/exporting CSV or Excel data, documenting provenance, and performing simple data-quality checks. Ability to follow data-governance protocols inside a corporate environment.

All Bloomberg data will be supplied by the instructional team, so applicants do not need their own Terminal access during the course. Participants should bring a Google account (for Colab/Drive) and reliable internet access for the live sessions.
